# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Kid-friendly activities

1. Shared responsive activity picker, support settings, navigation, safe local storage.
2. Robust drawing/coloring engine and age-fit creative prompts.
3. Learning content, tracing validation, counting/reasoning challenges.
4. Browser and unit QA, independent review, documentation, reviewable delivery.

Success: every original capability retained; all activities accessible at all age defaults; recoverable artwork; complete trace paths required; no horizontal viewport overflow across the tested phone/tablet matrix; automated checks pass in Chromium and WebKit.

## Phase 2 — Reliable delivery and 24 meaningful activities

1. Standalone bundled app and offline reload cache, with regression for first entry after network loss.
2. Twenty-four categorized direct activity cards and fourteen new age-adjustable games.
3. Complete each game, inspect actual delivered browser, expand phone/tablet QA, update draft PR.

Success: every visible card launches and plays, the downloaded HTML works independently of the preview server, and the delivered preview retains activities after network loss.
