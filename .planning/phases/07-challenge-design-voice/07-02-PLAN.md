# Distinct age-adaptive visual design

## Objective
Make the play surface clear, attractive and distinctive for every family on iPhone and iPad.

## Tasks
1. Add original SVG family illustrations, simpler shared navigation, mature older-child styling independent of support adjustment, and age-specific creative challenges.
2. Visually inspect every family at preschool, early-primary and older-primary ages on phone/tablet; check touch targets, overflow, legibility and drawing safe areas.

## Success criteria
All 34 modes are retained. Every family has a distinct visual identity. Puzzle area takes precedence over repetitive help chrome. Controls remain accessible and at least 48px where touch is required.
