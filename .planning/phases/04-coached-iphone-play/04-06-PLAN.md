# App Store privacy, parent controls and listing

## Objective

Complete the three release-preparation items authorized by the owner: a public and offline in-app privacy policy, a parental gate before external actions, and an accurate store listing with iPhone/iPad screenshots. Preserve local signing settings. App Store upload/submission and physical-device testing are outside this increment.

## Tasks

1. Add responsive privacy/support pages and embed the same policy in the offline app. Gate every picture export and allowlisted external destination in both browser and native app. Verify cancellation, wrong answers, direct bridge requests, fresh approvals and navigation/background cleanup.
2. Prepare store metadata and genuine native screenshots at accepted iPhone/iPad dimensions. Verify generated bundle consistency, native and browser regressions, and document owner-only account/metadata fields without inventing contact details.

## Success criteria

- A family can read the complete policy without a network connection or parent challenge.
- No share sheet, download preview or external destination opens before a fresh parent check; cancellation preserves artwork.
- Production privacy/support URLs resolve to the prepared documents after publication.
- Store copy accurately represents all 30 activities, local storage and optional speech, with no unsupported educational or privacy promises.
- Final screenshots show the actual native app and record its bundle fingerprint and image dimensions.
- Owner signing configuration stays byte-for-byte unchanged; changes are tested and delivered for review.
