# Phase 4 — Guided play and an offline iPhone app

## Objective
Offer 30 meaningful activities with individual starting difficulty for ages 2–10, useful coaching, and a simple installable iOS project. Evaluate real game interactions through age-configured playthroughs; do not claim observed child enjoyment.

## Plan 01 — Play and coaching
1. Add six distinct spatial, sequencing, pattern and sharing games. Scale existing mechanics by exact age and provide actionable hints and recovery.
2. Add a shared coach, per-activity challenge adjustment, exact-age selection and age-specific drawing prompts. Preserve offline delivery and artwork.

## Plan 02 — Native delivery
1. Bundle the self-contained app into a SwiftUI/WKWebView iPhone/iPad app, with native drawing sharing and persistent local progress.
2. Build and exercise the app in the iPhone simulator. Provide Xcode source and clear personal-device signing instructions.

## Plan 03 — Verification
1. Complete every activity across the age settings in touch-oriented browsers; exercise mistakes, hints, success, navigation and persistence. Inspect representative phone/tablet layouts.
2. Record game-by-game challenge/enjoyment hypotheses, actual verification evidence and limits. Deliver a reviewable PR and updated standalone/source artifacts.

## Success criteria
All 30 activities load from the offline bundle and complete meaningful rounds. Ages 2–10 have distinct difficulty configurations, with user-controlled support. Coaching changes are recoverable and never erase artwork. Chromium/WebKit regression tests and native simulator tests pass. The iOS app launches without a server. No App Store submission, signing account change or production merge is implied.
