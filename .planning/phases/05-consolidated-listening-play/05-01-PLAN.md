# Consolidated activities and listening play

## Objective

Replace duplicate home activities with meaningful practice families and add actual offline listening challenges, preserving all existing art, exercises, progress, old routes and iPhone/iPad behavior.

## Tasks

1. Consolidate the 30 home cards into 17 existing-content families and four listening families (21 total). Add one consistent mode selector, align family titles and mode-specific coaching, preserve legacy links and saved practice. Keep the original visual sequence exercise as explicit Picture practice inside Melody echo.
2. Implement four distinct real-audio games: timbre matching, pitch contour, melody recall and relative drum rhythm. Provide starting steps for every age2–10, explicit Listen/replay, optional model hints, recoverable mistakes, independent sound controls and dependable cancellation. Use generated Web Audio; no microphone or remote audio dependency.
3. Verify mode and age coverage, offline delivery, actual nonzero audio rendering, native trusted taps and compact/tablet layouts. Refresh native bundle and current store assets/copy; retain previous reports as versioned evidence. Preserve owner signing and personal simulator data.

## Success criteria

- A home card represents a distinct activity family. Related exercises are visible modes with the correct Coach context.
- All 30 original modes remain reachable; saved drawings/progress and legacy links work.
- Four listening games require successfully played sound before awarding a listening result. Failed playback, mute, navigation or backgrounding cannot silently score an unheard round.
- Child-sized controls stay reachable on phone/tablet in both orientations. Sound starts from an intentional tap and ends when leaving/interruption occurs.
- Tests distinguish signal/gesture verification from physical-speaker audibility and observed child enjoyment. Store claims/images match the delivered version.
