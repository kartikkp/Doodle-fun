# Hands-on learning and free music

## Objective
Add three distinct touch-first learning families plus open-ended beat creation, preserving all existing activities.

## Tasks
1. Implement Mirror Mosaic, Balance Workshop and Measure & Pour with meaningful age 2–10 progressions, explicit Check, reversible manipulation and requested hints.
2. Add free Beat Maker with age-adjusted step grids, instruments, tempo, saved patterns and explicit playback/cancellation; creative modes remain ungraded.
3. Verify generators, complete actual playthroughs, invalid actions, offline recovery and compact iPhone/iPad layouts; integrate catalog, coaching and original illustrations.

## Success criteria
24 activity families / 38 modes remain playable offline. New touch games need no sound, score committed checks rather than exploration, and accept valid solutions. Beat creation reuses existing instrument playback, stops on background/navigation, and never autoplays.
