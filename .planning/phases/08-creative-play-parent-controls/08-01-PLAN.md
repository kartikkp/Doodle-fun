# Drawing for every age and touch-friendly tracing

## Objective
Keep creativity central, progressively offer richer art tools, support Pencil-only drawing, and make long words practical to trace on phones.

## Tasks
1. Preserve existing artwork/history/export while adding age-appropriate brush, shape, opacity and symmetry tools; filter palm/finger input in explicit Pencil-only mode.
2. Add enlarged letter-focused word tracing using canonical ink/scoring coordinates and verify phone/tablet layout, actual gestures, cancellation and whole-word completion.

## Success criteria
Drawing is prominent in All Activities at every age. Finger remains the default, Pencil controls remain touchable, and progress/art survive age and orientation changes. Long words such as evidence remain readable and traceable without relaxing correct-path evaluation. Hardware Pencil/palm behavior is recorded separately from synthetic tests.
