# Meaningful medals and progression

## Objective
Reward learning mastery through short untimed sets without grading open-ended creativity.

## Tasks
1. Implement a versioned attempt ledger and best Bronze/Silver/Gold medals keyed by mode, selected age and content step, preserving older completion stores.
2. Instrument every learning mode with honest mistake/hint/completion boundaries; expose progress and a recommended next step after Gold.

## Success criteria
Perfect independent sets earn Gold, one supported/retried round earns Silver, completed sets earn Bronze. Content step fixes set size; complex boards use shorter sets. Normal exploration, replay, instructions, partial strokes and accessibility do not count as mistakes. Restart/reload cannot erase retries, duplicate completions cannot farm rewards, and best medals are never downgraded. Drawing/coloring/free composition/free mosaic are ungraded.
