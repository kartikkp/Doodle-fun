A LITTLE HELP

Help & support

Find their pace

Choose a starting age from 2 to 10. Inside any activity, open Coach for a first step, a strategy and a hint. Use “A little easier” or “A little harder” to adjust that activity.

Keep a picture

In Doodle studio, draw freely or choose Coloring pages, then tap Save. A grown-up answers a question before choosing where the picture goes. Cancel at any point to keep drawing. Undo brings back the previous step.

Listening games

Choose Listen on the home screen for sound matching, pitch, melody, and rhythm games. Tap Listen inside the game to begin. Use your device’s media-volume buttons for game sounds and spoken instructions. Check device media volume and connected speakers or headphones if sounds are quiet.

Hear the clues

Tap “Hear it” inside an activity or “Hear these tips” in Coach. Spoken help starts only when you ask. Check your device volume if you cannot hear the voice.

Kid-safe play

Optional first-time setup and Grown-ups offer kid-safe play. Your parent PIN protects returning to the activity menu and changing age or difficulty. Sharing and outside websites stay closed until a grown-up turns kid-safe play off. To prevent leaving Doodle Fun on iPhone or iPad, separately start Guided Access in the device’s accessibility settings. The app PIN cannot block the Home gesture or app switching.

Hints and parent PIN

In Grown-ups, leave hints available, turn them off, or ask for a parent PIN each round. You can also lock age and difficulty changes. Keep your recovery code: it resets controls without deleting artwork or medals. Instructions and question audio remain available.

Practice medals

Finish a short set to earn Bronze. Silver means all but one round independently correct; Gold means every round, without hints or a failed check. Memory exploration and creative play are not graded as mistakes. Medals are kept separately for each starting age and practice step.

Saved on this device

Settings, practice, medals, beat patterns and the latest drawing are saved locally. Browser and app copies are separate. Device backups may include this data. Save a picture you want to keep before removing the app or clearing browser data.

Need more help?

A grown-up can email pishahrodi+support@gmail.com. Our support website has troubleshooting steps and a link to open your email app. Nothing is sent automatically. Please do not include a child’s name, photo or other personal details in your message.

IPHONE & IPAD · FOR A GROWN-UP

Keep the device in Doodle Fun

Step 1 of 3

1. Set up in Apple Settings

The app PIN cannot stop the Home gesture or app switching. Doodle Fun cannot turn Guided Access on or open its Settings page for you.

Settings↓Accessibility↓Guided Access → On

Open the device’s Settings app yourself and follow this path. Under Passcode Settings → Set Guided Access Passcode, choose a Guided Access passcode. This is separate from your Doodle Fun PIN and your device unlock passcode. You may also enable Face ID or Touch ID here.

If a session is already active, end it using step 3 before opening Settings.

2. Return here and start a session

  1. Return to Doodle Fun, close this guide and choose an activity.
  2. Triple-click the side button on iPhone or top button on iPad. On a device with a Home button, triple-click Home instead. Choose Guided Access if the Accessibility Shortcuts panel appears.
  3. In Session Settings (or Options), leave Touch on so drawing works, and leave Software Keyboards on so you can enter the parent PIN. Leave Volume Buttons on if you want to adjust the game sound.
  4. Tap Start. If this app has previous Guided Access settings, the session may start immediately.

Triple-click does nothing? Check that Guided Access is on in Settings → Accessibility, then return to this app and try again.

3. End the device lock

Triple-click the same side, top, or Home button. On iOS or iPadOS 18 or earlier, double-click instead to pause. Authenticate with the Guided Access passcode or enabled Face ID / Touch ID; select Guided Access if a shortcut panel appears. Then tap End. Tap Resume to keep playing.

The Doodle Fun PIN only opens this app’s grown-up controls. It cannot end Apple’s Guided Access session. If the PIN keyboard is hidden, pause Guided Access and turn Software Keyboards on in Session Settings (or Options).

On the web, Guided Access locks Safari itself, not a single website. Browser controls remain separate from the Doodle Fun PIN.

Before handing over the device: Apple says Crash Detection and emergency calls are unavailable during Guided Access. End the session to use them. Keep the device with a grown-up.
