## Delivered behavior

- Drawing is first at every age. Marker and shapes arrive from age five; line, opacity and mirror tools from eight. Pencil-only input can reject touch before a palm claims the canvas. Long phone tracing items have a focused-letter view while retaining whole-word ink and grading.
- Mirror mosaic, Balance workshop and Measure & pour add silent touch learning. Older steps include diagonal reflections, indirect unknown-weight equations with piece constraints, and varied conservation problems requiring longer plans. Beat studio adds saved, ungraded composition with explicit playback.
- Parents choose hints On, Off or Ask a grown-up, with optional age/difficulty locking. A local salted PIN verifier and recovery verifier are stored, not plaintext secrets. Recovery preserves artwork and progress. Approval expires by round, route and inactivity; cached models are hidden when restricted. Essential instructions, requested question audio and sound replay remain accessible.
- Bronze, Silver and Gold record short learning sets separately by mode, chronological age and practice step. Gold requires every round clean and independent; Silver permits one imperfect round. Creative work is ungraded. Exploration, ordinary partial strokes, memory flips and Undo are not mistakes. Finite tracing sets offer an explicit fresh attempt after completion. The next difficulty step is suggested, never forced.
- The Numbers comparison triangle labels fit their illustration. Four new offline coaching clips bring the bundled collection to 120. Public support/privacy content describes the added local data accurately.
