## Defects found and corrected

- **Safe-area test readiness:** one focused CI run froze the initial CSS inset reading before WebKit delivered UIKit’s rotated geometry. All six later activity readings matched 62/62/20 and drawing paper was 137 points square and contained. The test now requires three matching frame samples of viewport dimensions and all four insets within five seconds, recording initial/change/final timing. Frame waits have bounded timer fallbacks; timeout fails with diagnostics. The existing 0.5-point equality and every control/paper containment assertion remain unchanged. The full native matrix passed at revision `a52187e`; its measured geometry matched initially at 4 ms and remained matched across three frames by 104 ms. The dedicated release run also passed: on the cold large phone, correct insets arrived by 1,169 ms and three matching frame samples completed by 1,228 ms; compact readiness completed by 1,238 ms. No tolerance or downstream assertion was relaxed.
- **Retired audio context revival:** WebKit could deliver a late running event after a retired context had closed. A context-local guard closes it again without interrupting a replacement. The original closure/scoring assertions remain; ten repeated WebKit runs passed after the fix.
- **Landscape inspiration row:** the prompt and refresh control could enter a notched phone's unsafe area. Padding now respects native insets; the layout test checks text and control bounds.
- **Support test geometry:** the button's center was at y841, outside its help dialog ending at y828. The helper now scrolls using the actual dialog/window intersection. Production approval behavior was unchanged; full native gate assertions passed on large and compact phones.
- **Rainbow clouds:** a white rectangle erased their lower outlines and allowed fill into the exterior. Removing it preserves the boundaries. Before/after probes prove both leaks at 256- and 1536-pixel canvas sizes in both engines. Browser tests verify both clouds, unchanged exterior/band/opposite cloud, and exact whole-canvas Undo. The native nine-page test includes equivalent checks and passed in final compact CI 35544788457.
- **Compact drawing canvas:** the 667×375 viewport left 247 points below navigation, but the 320-point drawing minimum pushed the paper bottom to420. Removing that minimum and reclaiming spacing on short landscape phones preserves existing 44–48-point controls and the inspiration text. The first correction exposed a 402-point notched-phone gap, covered by the final 430-point breakpoint. Final browser geometry is 130-point square paper on 667×375, and 136-point paper on 874×402 with native 62-point side/21-point bottom insets represented in CSS. Neither browser page overflows. Final native measurements were 130-point compact and 137-point large paper, with full safe-area containment and no document overflow.
- **Navigation padding changes:** the app measured border-box heights but observed content boxes. A fixed-width padding-only change left the CSS height at 130 while the actual bars measured 114, shrinking the paper to 114. Observing border boxes keeps both measurements at 114 and restores 130-point paper in both engines. Ordinary desktop rotation alone did not reproduce the missed update; the separate padding regression proves the contract defect without overstating the native delivery sequence.

No paper-visibility or artwork-recovery assertion was weakened. The native layout test measures before scrolling and now requires a square canvas over 120 points, complete safe-area containment, current main bounds and no document overflow. Earlier fixes to malformed sound preferences, legacy-tab Coach/difficulty alignment, and background listening recovery remain covered.
