Privacy policy

Privacy policy · Last updated September 20, 2026

Doodle Fun is a drawing and activity app for children and the grown-ups who play alongside them. The native iPhone and iPad app works without an account and does not send personal data, artwork, or practice progress to the developer. A grown-up can separately choose to contact support by email, as explained below.

What the app does not collect

We do not collect personal data through the native app. There are no developer-operated accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking tools, or cloud-sync services. We do not ask for a child's name, birthdate, email address, location, contacts, microphone recordings, or photos. We do not sell or share personal data from the app with advertisers or data brokers.

What stays on your device

Doodle Fun saves a small amount of information locally so you can return to your activities:

  • Your preferences, including the chosen age, activity difficulty, and read-aloud setting. The age choice is a starting point for activity difficulty, not a birthdate or a verified age.
  • Practice progress, such as completed challenges and activity achievements.
  • The current drawing or coloring draft, so you can continue creating later.

This information is kept in the app's storage on that device. In the web version, it is kept in that browser's site storage. Doodle Fun does not provide its own backup, account recovery, or synchronization between devices.

Keeping and removing local data

Local information remains until it is replaced or removed, or the operating system or browser clears the app's storage. To remove the native app's local information, use Delete App on your iPhone or iPad; offloading an app can retain its data. To remove the web version's local information, clear Doodle Fun's site data in your browser. Save any pictures you want to keep first.

Your operating system or device-backup service may include local app data in device or cloud backups, depending on your settings. Those backups are controlled by your device and backup provider, not by Doodle Fun. Manage or delete backups separately through that provider. Pictures you have saved or shared outside the app also remain in those destinations until you remove them there.

Listening games and game sound

Listening games generate tones and percussion sounds on your device after you choose a playback control, such as Listen, Hear, or a tone pad. They do not use the microphone, record anyone's voice, download sound recordings, or send listening responses to the developer. Completed-practice progress and the optional read-aloud setting are stored locally. Use device media volume to adjust game sounds; Read aloud controls spoken help.

Optional read-aloud

On iPhone and iPad, read-aloud uses Apple's system speech voices to speak the app's instructions. It does not record your voice or use the microphone. In the web version, speech is provided by your browser and operating system; their voice services and privacy settings apply. Doodle Fun does not operate a speech server or receive the spoken text or audio.

Saving and sharing pictures

When you choose to save or share a picture, the app creates a PNG image. A grown-up check comes before opening the system share sheet or the browser's sharing or download options. You choose whether to continue and where the picture goes. Doodle Fun does not upload it to a developer server.

Any app, person, or service you choose as a destination handles the picture under its own privacy practices. Check the picture before sharing, especially if it includes a child's name or other identifying details.

The website and web version

The Doodle Fun website and web version are hosted on GitHub Pages. Loading those pages makes a network request to GitHub, which may process information such as your IP address and request details under the . We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies to these pages.

The native iPhone and iPad app includes its activities and this policy on the device; opening them does not load the GitHub Pages website. Following an external support or policy link opens a website after a grown-up check, and that website's privacy practices apply.

Getting help without sharing children's information

A grown-up can email pishahrodi+support@gmail.com or visit our for help and an email link. Please do not send children's names, birthdates, contact details, photos, or artwork that identifies them. A description of the problem, device model, operating-system version, and steps to reproduce it is usually enough.

If you choose to email support, the developer receives your sender email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send, and uses them to respond and troubleshoot. Email services handle delivery and storage under their own privacy practices. This voluntary correspondence is separate from using the app: there is no in-app support form, and the app does not automatically attach artwork, progress, device identifiers, or other data to a message. You can contact the same address with a question or request about your support correspondence.

Developers can optionally report bugs through . GitHub issues are public. If you submit an issue, your message and GitHub profile information are handled by GitHub and visible to the developer and other readers; GitHub's privacy and retention practices apply. Do not include children's identifying information in a public report.

Changes and questions

We will update this policy if Doodle Fun's data practices change and show the revision date above. For privacy questions, a grown-up can email pishahrodi+support@gmail.com without including personal information about a child.
A LITTLE HELP

Help & support

Find their pace

Choose a starting age from 2 to 10. Inside any activity, open Coach for a first step, a strategy and a hint. Use “A little easier” or “A little harder” to adjust that activity.

Keep a picture

In Doodle studio, draw freely or choose Coloring pages, then tap Save. A grown-up answers a question before choosing where the picture goes. Cancel at any point to keep drawing. Undo brings back the previous step.

Listening games

Choose Listen on the home screen for sound matching, pitch, melody, and rhythm games. Tap Listen inside the game to begin. Use your device’s media-volume buttons for game sounds; Read aloud controls only spoken instructions. Check device media volume and connected speakers or headphones if sounds are quiet.

Hear the clues

Turn on Read aloud, then tap “Hear it” or open Coach. Check your device volume if you cannot hear the voice. Voice availability depends on your device.

Saved on this device

Settings, practice progress and the latest drawing are saved locally. Browser and app copies are separate. Device backups may include this data. Save a picture you want to keep before removing the app or clearing browser data.

Need more help?

A grown-up can email pishahrodi+support@gmail.com. Our support website has troubleshooting steps and a link to open your email app. Nothing is sent automatically. Please do not include a child’s name, photo or other personal details in your message.
