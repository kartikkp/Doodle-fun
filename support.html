Common questions

Tap a question to open its answer.

How do I save a picture?
  1. In Draw or Coloring, tap Save.
  2. Ask a grown-up to complete the multiplication question, then tap Continue.
  3. On iPhone or iPad, choose an available destination in the share sheet, such as Save Image or Save to Files. In a browser, use the sharing options or the picture preview's Download PNG button.

Sharing creates a PNG picture. Cancelling leaves your drawing in the app. If saving to Photos is unavailable, check the device's permissions or choose another destination. A new grown-up check is required each time you share.

Is my drawing saved automatically?

The app normally keeps your current drawing or coloring draft on this device. Look for Draft saved on this device. Very detailed pictures or limited storage may prevent the draft from being kept; the app will ask you to save a PNG instead.

There is one current draft, not a gallery or a cloud account. Use Save to keep a picture before starting a new one. Undo and Redo help during the current drawing session, but are not a backup.

How do I adjust an activity?

Choose an age on the home screen, or use Grown-ups to change the starting settings. All 30 activities remain available. Open Coach inside an activity for tips and an easier or harder practice step for that game.

The age choice is guidance, not an assessment. Younger children may enjoy exploring alongside a grown-up, especially in word and number activities. Follow the child's interest and adjust whenever it helps.

How does read-aloud work?

Turn on read-aloud with the speaker button or in Grown-ups, then tap Hear it where available. Coach also has a button to read its tips. Read-aloud is optional and does not record anyone's voice.

On iPhone and iPad, it uses system speech voices. If you cannot hear it, check the device volume and connected headphones or speakers, then try the read-aloud button again. Browser voice availability depends on the browser and operating system.

Does Doodle Fun need the internet?

The native iPhone and iPad app includes all 30 activities and works offline. No account or sign-in is needed. Opening an external support website needs an internet connection; a sharing destination may also need one.

The web version needs a connection for its first visit. A browser may keep an offline copy, but that depends on its settings and available storage.

Why is there a grown-up check?

Saving or sharing a picture and opening external websites pause for a grown-up to answer a multiplication question. Enter the answer to the question shown and tap Continue. Cancel returns to the app.

The check is requested for each action and is not remembered. Leaving the app while a check is pending cancels it. The privacy policy can be read inside the app without completing a check.

Can I move or recover my progress?

Settings, practice progress, and the current draft are stored locally. They do not sync between devices, or between the website and native app. There is no developer-held copy to restore. Keep important pictures by saving them outside the app.

Your device's backup service may include app data, depending on your backup settings. Recovery from those backups is handled by your device or backup provider.

How do I remove saved information?

Save pictures you want to keep first. On iPhone or iPad, use Delete App to remove Doodle Fun and its local app data; offloading can retain that data. For the web version, clear Doodle Fun's site data in your browser.

Manage device or cloud backups separately. Deleting the app does not remove pictures already saved to Photos, Files, or another sharing destination. See the privacy policy for more detail.

Something stopped working. What should I try?

If possible, save the current picture first. Return to the home screen and reopen the activity. If the problem continues, close and reopen the app, and check for an available update.

Avoid deleting the app or clearing site data as a first troubleshooting step: that can remove your local draft and progress. If you contact the project, include the activity name and the steps that led to the problem.

Still need a hand?

A grown-up can report a problem, ask a question, or suggest an idea through the Doodle Fun project on GitHub.

GitHub issues are public. Please do not include a child's name, birthdate, contact details, photos, or artwork that identifies them.

Helpful details to include

  • The activity and what you were trying to do.
  • What happened and what you expected.
  • Your device model and operating-system version, or browser name and version.
  • The app version, if you know it.
Open GitHub support ↗

A GitHub account is needed to post. Reading existing issues does not require submitting any information. GitHub's own privacy practices apply.
